UT320i Web Bluetooth Explorer

Connect a UNI-T UT320i pipe clamp, inspect its BLE services, and capture frames to reverse-engineer the protocol.

This browser does not support Web Bluetooth. Use Chrome or Edge on desktop/Android (or the Bluefy browser on iOS). The page must be served over HTTPS or localhost.
